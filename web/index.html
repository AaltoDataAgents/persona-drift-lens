      How this works

      The model can write as a teacher, a counsellor, or a fictional character. We compare its internal activity during a reply with patterns saved from earlier role prompts and neutral instructions. The drift chart shows that comparison for each reply. We call the reference built from neutral instructions the default Assistant.

        Read the research

        • Lu and colleagues describe the Assistant Axis and how conversations drift away from it. We adapted their way of measuring it for this demonstration. Lu, C., Gallagher, J., Michala, J., Fish, K., & Lindsey, J. (2026). The Assistant Axis: Situating and Stabilizing the Default Persona of Language Models. arXiv:2601.10387. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10387
        • Chen and colleagues describe how to extract persona vectors from prompted replies. We used their method to build the Agreement gauge. Chen, R., Arditi, A., Sleight, H., Evans, O., & Lindsey, J. (2025). Persona Vectors: Monitoring and Controlling Character Traits in Language Models. arXiv:2507.21509. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21509
        • Li and colleagues examine how models follow their initial instructions across a conversation. Li, K., Liu, T., Bashkansky, N., Bau, D., Viégas, F., Pfister, H., & Wattenberg, M. (2024). Measuring and Controlling Instruction (In)Stability in Language Model Dialogs. arXiv:2402.10962. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10962
